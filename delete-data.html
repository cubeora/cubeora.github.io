Delete your CubeOra data

This page explains how to delete the data that the game CubeOra (developer: CubeOra) stores about you.

What gets deleted

Your player ID, player name, country, avatar and all your scores on the World, Country, Daily and Endless leaderboards. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Option 1 · In the game (instant)

  1. Open CubeOra.
  2. Tap the gear icon (Settings) › Privacy & data › Delete my data.
  3. Confirm. Your online data is deleted immediately. You can also tick "Also reset my game progress on this phone".

You can also do this from your profile (top left of the home screen) › Delete my data.

Option 2 · By email (if you no longer have the game)

Email cubeora.games@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my CubeOra data" and your player name and country as shown on the leaderboard (and your player ID if you have it: Settings › Privacy & data). We delete the data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Game progress

Levels, coins and purchases are saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the game removes them.
