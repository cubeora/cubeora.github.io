Privacy policy

This policy explains what the game CubeOra ("the game"), made by CubeOra ("we"), collects and why. In short: we keep only what the leaderboards need, the only ads are short videos you choose to watch, and we do not track you across other apps.

1. What stays on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, badges), settings and profile are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

2. What we collect for the leaderboards

When the online leaderboards are available, to show the World, Country, Daily and Endless leaderboards the game sends the following to our leaderboard server:

Your player name, country, avatar and scores are visible to other players. Please don't use your real full name if you don't want it shown. The server also needs your IP address to deliver the data; we do not store it with your scores.

The leaderboard server is run for us by Supabase Inc. (database hosting). Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We do not sell or share this data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Optional sign-in

You can play without signing in. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone), the game reads your public player name from that service to fill in your name. We do not receive your email address or password. Google's and Apple's own privacy policies apply to those services.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The game keeps a record of what you bought on your device so it can deliver it.

5. Optional video ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one, for example to get a free hint or hammer, extra moves, or double coins after a level. There are no banner or pop-up ads, and players who buy "No Ads" get these rewards without any video. These videos are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.).

When an ad is requested, Google's ad software in the game collects, as described in Google's own disclosures: your IP address (which can show your approximate location), your device's advertising ID or app set ID, information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, and diagnostic data such as crashes and app performance. Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. We do not receive this data ourselves and do not combine it with your leaderboard data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, the game first shows Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & data › Ad privacy choices. On iPhone the game does not ask for permission to track you, so ads there are not personalised with Apple's advertising identifier. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google › Ads). Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy. The game has no other analytics or tracking.

6. How long we keep data

Leaderboard data is kept until you delete it. Daily Challenge scores are deleted after 30 days.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can change your name, country and avatar at any time in the game (tap your profile, top left). To delete everything we store about you, open Settings › Privacy & data › Delete my data, or see Delete my data for other ways. Depending on where you live (for example under Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law, Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law or the EU GDPR) you also have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing; contact us at the address below.

8. Children

The game is suitable for everyone and contains no chat or messaging; the optional video ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them beyond the game-made player ID and the scores described above. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a player name, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date below. Important changes will also be shown in the game.
